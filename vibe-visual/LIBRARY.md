## PATTERN QUICK-PICK GUIDE

### P9 — Speech Bubble Grid (3×3 bubbles, mỗi cái 1 hướng mũi tên)
**Dùng cho:** Multi-perspective, noise, conflicting advice, social pressure, đám đông

Concepts phù hợp nhất:
- "If you want to make the wrong decision, ask everyone" → 9 bubbles, 9 directions = chaos
- "Everyone, Someone" → bubbles scattered vs one focused
- "Nothing draws a crowd quite like a crowd" → bubbles clustering
- "The more you compare, the less you differentiate" → bubbles pointing at each other

### P10 — Venn / Overlap (2-3 circles intersecting)
**Dùng cho:** Intersection, both/and, overlap between concepts

Concepts phù hợp nhất:
- "Reason for being" → 4 circles (Ikigai)
- "Creativity before productivity" → creative circle + productive circle, overlap = flow
- "zero-sum, infinite-sum" → pie vs expanding circles
- "Know yourself as Nothing. Feel yourself as Everything." → dot inside huge circle

### P11 — Single Symbol (1 element dominant, minimal label)
**Dùng cho:** Strong singular concept, action, awareness

Examples: Arrow (Movement), Eye (Vision/Perspective), Dot (You are here), Exclamation (Realize)

### P12 — Typography-led (text IS the visual)
**Dùng cho:** Long quote cần emphasis, khi không có shape equivalent

Rule: Ít nhất 1 word phải larger/bolder. Max 3 font sizes. Uppercase monospace label.

## DESIGN NOTES từ VV Catalog

Sau khi phân tích 170 visuals, các principles sau emerge:

1. **Duality là pattern phổ biến nhất** (~40% visuals) — VV thường visualize bằng cách đặt 2 thứ cạnh nhau
2. **Quote ngắn → shape, quote dài → typography** — dưới 5 từ = geometry; trên 15 từ = text-dominant
3. **Số lẻ powerful hơn số chẵn** — 3 roles (Bull/Bear/Builder), 3 types of people, 9 bubbles
4. **Tên riêng / attribution tạo credibility** — Naval, Buffett, Picasso, Frankl gắn với quote
5. **Action words → dynamic shapes** — "Movement" dùng arrow, "Resistance" dùng weight, "Shoot" dùng target
6. **Vật lý học là metaphor số 1** — gravity, resistance, momentum, curves, vectors
